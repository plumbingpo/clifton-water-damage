Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Clifton Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Clifton using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Add our number, (978) 755-9643, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods

What a Professional Brings to Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Professional extraction also addresses moisture that you cannot see or reach with consumer tools. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.

When to Call for Water Extraction in Clifton

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
