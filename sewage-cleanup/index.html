Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Clifton Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Clifton, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Clifton

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
